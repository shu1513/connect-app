# How marketplaces and creator platforms set the rules of buyer-seller messaging (as of November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-14. About 25 searches and fetches. Many official help pages (Fiverr, Upwork) returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher, so some of their rules rest on search-result excerpts of those official pages, not a full read. Where a source is a forum, vendor blog or competitor, it is labelled.

## 1. Actual messaging rules per platform (pre-booking messaging, booking-tied vs ongoing, seller-initiated, paid messages, limits)

### Takeaway
There are roughly four patterns: (a) free pre-booking inquiry, with seller replies gated on the buyer writing first (Clarity, MentorCruise, Airbnb, Fiverr, Upwork); (b) no messaging until a booking exists (TaskRabbit; PAAQ); (c) every message is paid (Cameo DMs, Topmate Priority DM, Wisio, Passes tipped DMs); (d) messaging only for paying members or subscribers (Patreon, Passes member-only, Ko-fi supporters). Call/expert marketplaces almost all let the seller reply but not cold-message, and most tie chat to a request or booking.

### Cited Findings

**Cameo (celebrity videos, paid DMs, calls)**
- Talent choose whether to offer DMs; if "Send a message" is not on a profile there is no other way to message that talent through Cameo — [Cameo Help: How do I send a Cameo DM](https://help.cameo.com/en/articles/12004207-how-do-i-send-a-cameo-dm); [Cameo Help: video request vs DM](https://help.cameo.com/en/articles/15255492-what-s-the-difference-between-a-cameo-video-request-and-a-direct-message-dm) (help articles undated)
- Fans pay when they send each DM; DMs are non-refundable even if talent does not reply; replies not guaranteed. Talent can set the DM price to $0.00 under Advanced options to make DMs free — [Cameo Help: What's a Cameo Direct Message?](https://help.cameo.com/en/support/solutions/articles/43000661378-what-s-a-cameo-direct-message-)
- Charged per message, not per thread; only in the Cameo app; prices from ~$2 per message (2021 reporting) — [Business Insider, 2021](https://www.businessinsider.in/tech/how-to/how-to-use-cameo-to-purchase-a-recorded-video-personalized-direct-message-or-live-zoom-call-with-your-favorite-celebrity/articleshow/80123187.cms)
- Talent can also send a fan a DM from their Inbox (talent-initiated messaging exists) — [Cameo Help: Talent—how do I send a fan a DM](https://help.cameo.com/en/articles/12007771-talent-how-do-i-send-a-fan-a-direct-message)

**Intro (intro.co)**
- Model is pick an expert and book a 1:1 video call; some experts sell ongoing "plans" or chat access; experts can enable "Request a Booking" (propose times at least 7 days out). No evidence found of free pre-booking messaging — [intro.co expert profile example](https://intro.co/miketrigg); [intro.co for experts](https://intro.co/experts)

**Superpeer**
- Acquired by Skillshare March 2024; standalone site shut December 2024 (so no current rules) — [TubeFilter, 2024-03-15](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/03/15/skillshare-acquires-student-instructor-connection-platform-superpeer/amp/); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/caly-vs-superpeer); [Talkspresso (competitor)](https://talkspresso.com/learn/vs-superpeer)

**Clarity.fm (paid expert calls; closest analogue to Connect's "request then accept")**
- Members can click "Send a Message" on an expert's profile before requesting a call — [Clarity Help: send a message via the inbox](https://clarity.fm/help/articles/22/how-do-i-send-a-message-via-the-inbox)
- Key rule: "Experts can only reply to messages if you've messaged them first or have submitted a call request." After a call request, messaging is automatically enabled. Sent messages cannot be edited or deleted — [same page](https://clarity.fm/help/articles/22/how-do-i-send-a-message-via-the-inbox)
- Experts are encouraged to message members after booking asking for bullet points of topics/questions/background to make the call efficient — [Clarity Help: how can experts best prepare for a call](https://clarity.fm/help/articles/35/how-can-experts-best-prepare-for-a-call)

**MentorCruise (paid mentorship subscriptions)**
- Mentees can message mentors to ask questions before booking; a first message creates an "inquiry", which is a "message request"; the mentor's reply lets the conversation continue; mentors can close an inquiry at any time with no profile penalty — [MentorCruise Help: How can I respond to inquiries?](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/68-how-can-i-respond-to-inquiries)
- MentorCruise also sells a free/cheap "Introductory Call" session type as a pre-purchase step — [MentorCruise intro call page](https://mentorcruise.com/sessions/introductory-call/book/15795/)

**ADPList (free mentorship)**
- Booking-request based: a request not accepted or declined within 48 hours expires; repeated expiries trigger warning emails; cancellations within 3 hours of the session are tracked — [ADPList mentee and mentor standards](https://intercom.help/adplist-community/en/articles/9399656-mentee-and-mentor-standards)
- No published rule found about free pre-booking DMs; a mentor quoted says she likes a confirmation message before the call (opinion) — [Looppanel interview](https://looppanel.com/blog/adplist-emilie-mazurek)

**Topmate (creator/expert storefront)**
- "Priority DM" is a paid async message product: experts filter inbound by putting a price (or required questions) on the DM; a "pay what you want" option exists — [Topmate Help: What's a Priority DM?](https://topmate72420835211887570.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000111288-what-s-a-priority-dm-); [Setting up Priority DMs](https://topmate72420835211887570.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000111289-setting-up-using-priority-dms)
- Topmate's marketplace page lists ~37,162 Priority DM offerings; a directory page shows median ₹60 for AMA-style DMs — [Topmate marketplace](https://topmate.io/marketplace/priority-dm); [Topmate AMA DMs](https://topmate.io/experts/ama-sessions-dm)
- Uses described: study-abroad experts collecting applicant details, engineers screening referral requests — [Topmate Help: async work with Priority DMs](https://topmate72420835211887570.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000111290-do-async-work-with-priority-dms)

**Wisio (paid video answers)**
- Fan buys a single question; creator replies with a short unedited video (~3 min mobile, up to 7 min desktop); faster turnaround sold at a higher price — [Wisio Medium: how to answer requests](https://medium.com/wisio/wisio-how-to-answer-requests-bee5f789c3f5); [Wisio Medium: The What, Why, How](https://medium.com/wisio/wisio-the-what-the-why-the-how-a626ea3e2207); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3910399,00.html)

**Fiverr**
- Fiverr limits message volume to curb spam; on hitting the limit you wait 60 minutes before messaging users not already in your contacts — [Fiverr Help: Using your inbox effectively](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37030193818257) (via search excerpt)
- Integrity policy bans contacting users "with offers, questions, suggestions, or anything unrelated to their Gigs, requests, or orders" — [Fiverr Community Standards: Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity)
- Fiverr offers in-inbox Zoom calls, some eligible sellers even before an order — [Fiverr Help: Video calls](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011062838-Video-calls-Zoom-calls); [Fiverr community blog, 2026-03-05](https://community.fiverr.com/en/public/blogs/how-to-use-fiverrs-zoom-feature-to-close-more-sales-2026-03-05)
- Users can block others from messaging them — [Fiverr Help: Blocking a user](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37328117128209-Blocking-a-user-from-messaging-you)
- Community FAQ claims sellers cannot make first contact with buyers (community source, lower weight) — [r/Fiverr FAQ mirror](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/r/Fiverr/wiki/faq)

**Upwork**
- All pre-contract discussions must happen in Upwork Messages or Upwork video calls (Enterprise exceptions); no published pre-contract message-count limit found — [Upwork Help: Get to know each other before a contract](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/17995658941843--Get-to-know-each-other-before-a-contract) (search excerpt; page 403'd to fetcher); [Upwork Help: On-platform interviews](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511833-On-platform-Interviews)
- After a contract starts, parties may use any tool they wish — [same](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/17995658941843--Get-to-know-each-other-before-a-contract)

**Airbnb**
- Guests can message a host before booking. Policy: "all communications prior to booking must be on Airbnb"; no links pushing users off-platform; no off-platform discounts or rebooking — [Airbnb Help: off-platform policy (art. 2799)](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2799)
- Airbnb reviews messages and may "block potentially dangerous messages" containing "words or numbers that might include contact information" — [Airbnb Help art. 209](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/209)
- Airbnb's community policy page says an updated policy applies to reservations booked on or after November 20, 2026 (details not confirmed) — [Airbnb Community policy art. 4169](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/4169)

**Etsy**
- Buyers message sellers freely; House Rules ban unsolicited advertising/promotions, donation requests, spam and harassment in Messages, and contacting someone after they have asked you to stop — [Etsy House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/legal/community)
- Etsy temporarily suspends messaging after bursts of messages or identical/unsolicited messages; no published numeric limit; suspensions reported from days to a month (forum anecdotes) — [Etsy community thread](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/temporary-message-restriction/m-p/143148780/highlight/true); [Etsy community thread](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/your-messages-have-been-temporarily-disabled/m-p/138820704)
- Etsy told sellers to send promotional messages to past buyers, then retracted it days later as against its own policy — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-cyber-special-message/)

**TaskRabbit**
- Clients cannot contact a Tasker before booking, citing privacy; chat and calling open after hiring, inside the app — [TaskRabbit Support: Can I contact a Tasker before booking?](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035010172-Can-I-Contact-a-Tasker-Before-Booking-a-Task)

**Thumbtack**
- The customer submits a request; pros reach out via in-app messages and a per-lead proxy phone number that routes to the customer; pros may ask for direct contact but lose the "unresponsive customer" lead refund if they move off-platform before the customer replies (staff posts on community forum) — [Thumbtack community: Phone numbers](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/2198/phone-numbers); [Thumbtack Numbers Policy thread](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7597)
- Thumbtack's privacy policy says it may record, transcribe and track communications that go through its tracked channels — [Thumbtack Privacy Policy](https://www.thumbtack.com/privacy)

**OnlyFans / Fanvue / Passes / Patreon / Ko-fi / Fourthwall**
- OnlyFans: creators send mass messages (immediate or scheduled) and attach paid media to make pay-per-view messages; "priority" messages land in a separate tab (vendor/agency blogs, not official) — [Onlymonster blog](https://onlymonster.ai/blog/onlyfans-priority-message-meaning/); [Fanso blog](https://fanso.io/blog/how-to-send-mass-messages-on-onlyfans)
- OnlyFans 2021 report: private-message price capped at $100 (may be outdated) — [Input, 2021](https://www.inputmag.com/culture/onlyfans-tries-to-absolve-bella-thorne-of-responsibility-for-payment-policy-changes)
- Fanvue: subscriptions $3.99–$100/month; pay-to-view messages exist; no official per-message price range found — [Fanvue Help: settings](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/7860532-settings-full-overview); [Fanvue blog](https://blog.fanvue.com/what-is-fanvue)
- Passes: creators choose who can DM (anyone, active members only, tipped messages only, Premium); some memberships include free messages refreshing on a 30-day cycle, no rollover; after free messages run out fans must pay/tip or upgrade; creators can turn media messages off globally or per chat — [Passes Help: Message creators and DM limits](https://help.passes.com/article/d0992c36-message-creators-and-dm-limits)
- Patreon: creators can only DM existing or former paid members; DMs cannot be deleted — [Patreon Help (via search excerpt)](https://support.patreon.com/hc/es-es/articles/203913429); blocking — [Patreon Help: blocking](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/207982093)
- Ko-fi: creators can set messaging so only supporters whom the creator messaged first can message back — [Ko-fi Help: Direct messages](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016956178-Direct-Messages-on-Ko-fi)
- Fourthwall: creators can require a minimum tip for member messages — [Fourthwall: how DMs work](https://help.fourthwall.com/memberships/getting-started-with-memberships/how-direct-messages-work.md)

**Other**
- PAAQ (expert calls) does not support open messaging before a session is booked; a short message can be included in the booking — [PAAQ Help](https://intercom.help/paaq/en/articles/11452858-can-i-chat-with-someone-before-booking)

### Inferences
- The most common rule on call/expert marketplaces is "buyer writes first; seller can only reply," plus "booking request automatically opens a thread." Clarity states this explicitly; MentorCruise's "message request" is the same idea.
- Platforms where the seller's time is the product (Cameo, Topmate, Wisio, Passes) put a price on the message itself rather than limiting count.
- Platforms with high privacy/safety stakes for workers in the physical world (TaskRabbit) block pre-booking contact entirely.

### Gaps
- Calendly-style booking tools: no source found. (From general product knowledge, not verified here: Calendly has no invitee-host chat; it has booking-form questions and email notifications only.)
- Intro: no official statement on pre-booking messaging found.
- OnlyFans official rules (who can DM whom, whether non-subscribers can DM) not confirmed from an official page.
- Fanhouse: not researched (no results gathered). Upwork freelancer-initiated contact rules (Connects/proposals) not confirmed from a fetched page.
- Fiverr exact first-contact rule for sellers is disputed between official and community sources.

## 2. Stopping off-platform contact and payment (detection, masking, policy wording, penalties, effectiveness)

### Takeaway
Big marketplaces combine three tools: a written ban on pre-transaction contact sharing, automated filtering of contact details in messages, and penalties up to account removal. Evidence shows masking reduces but does not stop leakage, and it frustrates honest users (Thumbtack proxy numbers).

### Cited Findings
- Airbnb filters messages with "words or numbers that might include contact information"; off-platform payment loses AirCover protection — [Airbnb Help art. 209](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/209)
- Airbnb penalties: educate, warn, suspend or remove listings, suspend or remove accounts — [Airbnb Help art. 2799](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2799)
- Third-party guides: Airbnb's filter covers emails, links and messaging handles, and disguising (spaces, words for symbols) may still be caught; contact details appear in the reservation once confirmed (vendor blogs, not official) — [STR Specialist](https://strspecialist.com/how-to-send-number-on-airbnb); [Hospitable Help: message content warning](https://help.hospitable.com/en/articles/7882505-message-content-warning)
- Fiverr bans sharing email, phone or third-party messaging apps "with the sole purpose of conducting business off-platform"; all transactions must happen on Fiverr; users asked to report attempts — [Fiverr Community Standards: Off-platform policy](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/38728907371665); [Fiverr Help: Stay protected](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601)
- Upwork: sharing contact info before a contract can mean loss of talent badges and, in some cases, permanent account closure; both sides barred from asking for or sharing contact info pre-contract — [Upwork community staff post](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Important-Reminder-Policy-on-Sharing-Contact-Information/m-p/1586109); [Upwork Help: sharing own communication tools](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/40444093133331-Can-I-share-my-own-communication-tools-like-email-or-meeting-links)
- Claims of automated Upwork scanning and contract closure within 14 days of a flagged message come from a marketing blog, not Upwork — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-exclusive-contracts) (unverified)
- Thumbtack uses per-lead proxy numbers; pros complain they look like spam and lower conversion, some threaten to leave (forum opinion) — [Thumbtack community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7588/); [Thumbtack community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7213/)
- Thumbtack uses an incentive rather than a ban: staying on-platform until the customer replies keeps a lead refund — [Thumbtack community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7599/)
- Sharetribe and others argue the real defense is value (payments protection, reviews, insurance, scheduling), with masking as a supplement — [Sharetribe Academy](https://www.sharetribe.com/academy/how-to-discourage-people-from-going-around-your-payment-system/); [Sharetribe glossary, 2023-12-27](https://sharetribe.com/marketplace-glossary/disintermediation-platform-leakage)
- Leakage is worse when buyers care about a specific provider (non-commodity services) — [Rework marketplace library](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/marketplace-growth/marketplace-disintermediation)
- Academic: Airbnb disintermediation studied with mobile location data (Lin, Nian & Foutz); UCLA summary of whether renters and owners ditch the site — [UCLA Anderson Review](https://anderson-review.ucla.edu/a-renter-and-property-owner-meet-on-airbnb-will-they-ditch-the-site); a Kellogg working paper on disintermediation — [Kellogg PDF](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/faculty/bray/doc/disintermediation/disintermediation.pdf)
- Besedo (moderation vendor) lists harms of personal details in marketplace messages — [Besedo](https://besedo.com/blog/3-ways-personal-details-can-damage-your-marketplace/)

### Inferences
- For Connect, the call itself happens on-platform, so off-platform risk is mainly "pay me on Venmo / book me privately next time" after a first call. A filter on contact details plus a clear policy line copied from Airbnb/Upwork ("before and between bookings, keep all contact on Connect") is cheap and familiar.
- Over-aggressive masking (Thumbtack) annoys honest users; a soft warning on detected contact info may be friendlier for a small product than hard blocking.

### Gaps
- No published numbers on how much contact filtering reduces leakage on Airbnb/Fiverr/Upwork.
- Exact Fiverr warning text and penalty ladder not retrieved (page 403).
- Details of Airbnb's policy effective 2026-11-20 not confirmed.

## 3. Spam and harassment of creators through open inboxes, and designs that reduce it

### Takeaway
Open DMs to well-known women are a documented abuse channel; the main mitigations seen across products are message requests (accept before chat), paying to message, members-only or supporters-only DMs, replies-only, volume limits, blocking, and media-off toggles.

### Cited Findings
- CCDH "Hidden Hate" (2022): 8,717 DMs to five high-profile women over a month; about 1 in 15 broke Instagram's rules, including 125 image-based sexual abuse examples; Instagram failed to act on 90% of reported abuse (Meta disputed) — [CCDH](https://counterhate.com/blog/dm-abuse-instagram-negligent-over-misogynist-harassment-of-women-in-the-public-eye-study-finds/); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/viral/instagram-ignores-dm-abuse-study-rcna23271)
- Some women said they could not close message requests because they rely on them for press and business contacts — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/viral/instagram-ignores-dm-abuse-study-rcna23271)
- Instagram's filter (April 2021) moves flagged requests to a hidden folder; critics say keyword filters miss many threats — [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/825794/new-study-reveals-instagrams-failure-to-protect-women-from-abusive-dms)
- Designs in creator products: Topmate prices DMs to filter inbound — [Topmate Help](https://topmate72420835211887570.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000111288-what-s-a-priority-dm-); Passes member-only, tip-only, media-off per chat — [Passes Help](https://help.passes.com/article/d0992c36-message-creators-and-dm-limits); Ko-fi "only people I message first" — [Ko-fi Help](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016956178-Direct-Messages-on-Ko-fi); Patreon paid members only — [Patreon Help](https://support.patreon.com/hc/es-es/articles/203913429); Cameo paid per DM, non-refundable — [Cameo Help](https://help.cameo.com/en/support/solutions/articles/43000661378-what-s-a-cameo-direct-message-)
- Rate limits: Fiverr 60-minute cooldown after a message-volume limit for non-contacts — [Fiverr Help](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37030193818257); Etsy temporary suspensions for bursts — [Etsy community](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/temporary-message-restriction/m-p/143148780/highlight/true)
- Dating apps: Bumble's women-message-first rule was meant to cut unwanted messages but Bumble never published proof; in 2024 it added "Opening Moves", and has since dropped women-first so either side can message first but the chat opens only when the other person chooses to; Bumble reported a rise in mutual chat rate (no numbers) — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2232004/bumble-lets-either-person-send-first-message); [Mediaweek](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/technology/bumble-drops-women-first-messaging-rule); [Vice review](https://www.vice.com/en/article/bumble-dating-app-review)

### Inferences
- Connect's hosts are the "high-profile women in the public eye" case at small scale. A design where strangers cannot send free, unlimited, media-carrying messages (requests-only, text-only, booking-gated) greatly narrows the main abuse path the CCDH study describes. It does not remove it: someone who is allowed to write can still send an abusive or threatening text, which is why reporting and blocking are still needed.
- Pay-to-message also filters abuse, but Cameo's "non-refundable, no reply guaranteed" can feel unfair to buyers and adds payment/refund work.

### Gaps
- No quantitative evidence found that pay-to-message reduces harassment versus free requests.
- Hinge/dating like-limit specifics not confirmed.

## 4. What pre-call and post-call messaging is used for; effect on conversion

### Takeaway
On call marketplaces messaging is mainly used for: a pre-sale question ("can you help with X?"), sending topics and background before the call, logistics/rescheduling, and short follow-ups. No hard published data was found on whether messaging raises or lowers booking conversion.

### Cited Findings
- Clarity advises experts to message after booking asking for bullet-point topics, questions and background — [Clarity Help](https://clarity.fm/help/articles/35/how-can-experts-best-prepare-for-a-call); members ask about sending slides/PDFs before a call — [LaunchRock Q&A](https://www.launchrock.com/community/questions/4474/can-i-send-an-expert-a-slideshow-or-pdf-to-review-prior-to-a-call/answers/14696)
- Clarity members use the thread to change accepted calls (for example 15 to 45 minutes) — [Clarity Q&A](https://clarity.fm/questions/3202/how-to-i-change-my-tome-request-of-15-minutes-that-has-been-accepted-to-a-45)
- MentorCruise mentors use a "welcome message" field to set expectations on first contact — [MentorCruise Help: welcome message](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/84-using-the-welcome-message-field)
- ADPList mentor values a confirmation message before the call (opinion) — [Looppanel](https://looppanel.com/blog/adplist-emilie-mazurek)
- Topmate experts use paid DMs to collect details or screen requests before a call — [Topmate Help](https://topmate72420835211887570.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000111290-do-async-work-with-priority-dms)
- Fiverr promotes pre-order Zoom calls in the inbox "to close more sales" (platform's own claim, no numbers) — [Fiverr community blog, 2026-03-05](https://community.fiverr.com/en/public/blogs/how-to-use-fiverrs-zoom-feature-to-close-more-sales-2026-03-05)
- Fiverr help guidance on answering potential clients quickly (response behaviour tied to sales) — [Fiverr Help: Best practices for client management](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010736117-Answering-potential-clients)
- Thumbtack pros say masked numbers hurt their conversion (forum opinion) — [Thumbtack community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7588/)

### Inferences
- A booking-request note field (as PAAQ does) covers most "agree the topic" needs without an open inbox.
- A thread tied to each booking covers rescheduling, material sharing and a short follow-up window.

### Gaps
- No public A/B data found on pre-booking messaging vs booking conversion for call marketplaces.

## 5. Lessons for a small pre-launch product; cases of opening then restricting (or the reverse)

### Takeaway
The simplest well-tested rule set for a call marketplace is Clarity's: buyer can send a first message or a booking request; host can only reply; a booking opens a thread; no contact details before booking. Documented reversals exist mainly in dating (Bumble loosening its rule) and Etsy (policy-vs-advice conflict), not in call marketplaces.

### Cited Findings
- Clarity's one-sentence rule ("experts can only reply if you messaged first or submitted a call request") is an example of an easy-to-explain rule — [Clarity Help](https://clarity.fm/help/articles/22/how-do-i-send-a-message-via-the-inbox)
- TaskRabbit's "no contact until booking" is the strictest and simplest — [TaskRabbit Support](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035010172-Can-I-Contact-a-Tasker-Before-Booking-a-Task)
- Bumble moved from a strict rule (women first) to a looser accept-to-open model after user research — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2232004/bumble-lets-either-person-send-first-message); [Mediaweek](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/technology/bumble-drops-women-first-messaging-rule)
- Etsy publicly reversed advice encouraging seller-initiated promotional messages — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-cyber-special-message/)
- Thumbtack's proxy-number system drew a sustained pro backlash asking for real numbers back — [Thumbtack Numbers Policy thread](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7597)
- Meta shut its Cameo-like "Super" app in Feb 2023; Superpeer was absorbed into Skillshare in 2024 (both show how fragile this category is, not messaging-specific) — [TubeFilter, 2022-12-16](https://www.tubefilter.com/2022/12/16/meta-super-cameo-meet-and-greet-platform-shut-down/); [TubeFilter, 2024-03-15](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/03/15/skillshare-acquires-student-instructor-connection-platform-superpeer/amp/)

### Inferences
- Rule sets that are easy to explain and police at small scale, in order of simplicity: (1) no chat until a booking request; request includes a note; thread per booking. (2) Plus one free first message per customer per host before booking, host reply opens the thread (Clarity/MentorCruise style). (3) Paid DMs as a separate product (Cameo/Topmate) — more revenue, more refund and dispute work.
- Starting strict and loosening later is easier for users to accept than starting open and restricting, judging by the backlash to restrictions (Thumbtack) and the positive framing of loosening (Bumble); this is an inference, not a documented study.

### Gaps
- No documented case found of a call/consulting marketplace that launched fully open messaging and later restricted it (or vice versa) with published reasons.
- No relevant Lenny's Newsletter, a16z or Everything Marketplaces pieces on messaging rules surfaced in searches.
