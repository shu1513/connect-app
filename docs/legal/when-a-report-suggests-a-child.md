# When a report suggests a child, or someone being exploited

A draft of a one-page procedure for the founder, written on 26 November 2026 by the builder for
plan 49 (`plans/49-messaging.md`). **It is not legal advice and no lawyer has checked it.** A
lawyer must correct it before Connect opens to real people, and the steps marked LAWYER are open
questions. They are also in `docs/for-the-lawyer.md`, section 8.

**Why it exists.** Once people can write private messages to each other through Connect, the
law of the United States expects Connect to report certain things when it comes to know of them,
and to keep what it reported. It does not expect Connect to search messages for them. The app is
built that way: nobody at Connect sees a conversation unless one of its two people reports a
message.

## Before anything happens (once)

1. **Register with NCMEC** (the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children), so that there
   is an account to send a report through its CyberTipline.
2. **Choose one email address for the police and the courts** and publish it. LAWYER: where.
3. **Decide who may read reports.** Today that is the founder alone.

## When a report comes in

The reports that need this page are the ones marked URGENT with the reason "Someone under 18",
and any report, whatever its reason, in which you find one of these:

- a picture or a description of the sexual abuse of a child;
- an adult trying to get a child to meet, to send pictures, or to keep a secret;
- someone being sold, forced or threatened for sex or for money.

Then, in this order:

1. **Read the report.** On the "Reports and safety" page, read the reporter's note. If the
   report was made from a message, press "Read the kept messages". That press is written into
   the record of admin actions by the app. Do not copy the messages anywhere else.
2. **Pause the account** of the person reported, from the same page. Do not warn them, do not
   write to them about the report, and do not tell them who reported.
3. **Do not delete anything.** The app keeps what was kept with the report. It does not delete
   reports.
4. **If someone is in danger right now, call the police first** (911 in the United States).
5. **Send a report to NCMEC's CyberTipline** as soon as you can. Say what you know: who (the
   names and email addresses on the two accounts), when, and what was written. LAWYER: exactly
   what must be in it, and how fast; whether a report about someone outside the United States
   goes somewhere else too.
6. **Write down that you reported:** the day, the report's number at Connect (R-…) and the
   number NCMEC gives you. LAWYER: where this is kept.
7. **Keep what you reported for at least one year** from the day of your report to NCMEC. Do not
   close or wipe the account's records before then. LAWYER: the exact period, and what "keeping
   it safely" asks of us.
8. **Close the report in the app** only after that, with a note that names no detail of what was
   written.

## If the person might be under 13

Close the account at once, whatever else is true. Connect is not for children, and the law treats
what we hold about a child under 13 differently. LAWYER: what else that law asks.

## If someone asks for an intimate picture to be taken down

Messages on Connect are text only, so this is about a host's diary. A person can ask through the
"Copyright and complaints" page. The picture must be gone within 48 hours of a valid request.
LAWYER: what a valid request is, and whether a description in a message falls under this law.

## What the app does and does not do

- It keeps, with a report made from a message, the reported message and up to 100 messages before
  it and 50 after it.
- It deletes a message 12 months after it was sent. It never deletes what is kept with a report.
- It records every look at kept messages.
- It does **not** send anything to NCMEC, the police or anyone else. That is done by a person.
