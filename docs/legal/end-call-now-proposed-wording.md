# "End call now": proposed wording

Written on 26 November 2026 by the builder, for plan 51 (`plans/51-end-call-now.md`, decision 24).
**Nothing here is in force, and none of it was written or checked by a lawyer.** It is proposed
wording for four of the documents in `src/legal/documents/`, for a lawyer to read and correct.

**Why it is here and not in the app.** A change to one of these documents is a new version, and a
new version sends every account back to the welcome step to accept again. That should happen
once, on purpose, with the lawyer's words, and together with the other changes that are waiting
(`docs/legal/messages-proposed-wording.md`, `docs/for-the-lawyer.md`). The documents in the app
are drafts and say so on every page. Until the new versions are in, **they describe the button as
it was** ("End call and report", with the money waiting for our review), and the app is ahead of
them: the button is "End call now" and the money settles by itself.

The open questions behind this wording are in `docs/for-the-lawyer.md`, section 25.

## What the app does (so the wording can be checked against it)

- In a call each person has one button, "End call now". It ends the call for both at once, and
  nobody can come back in. Before it does, the app asks once and says what it costs that person.
- A customer is also told when he books, in the words he ticks (version "2026-11-d"), and on the
  confirm-and-pay step: "If you end the call yourself with 'End call now', you pay for your whole
  booked time: the time the host was in the call, and the rest of your booking. If you end it
  before its booked start, that is charged as cancelling it at that moment would be." A booking made
  under the earlier words is charged by the host's time, whoever ends the call.
- Right after, one screen offers "Report", "Block" and "Neither". Reporting blocks. Blocking also
  cancels the two people's other calls that are still to come, at no charge, and ends any
  membership or monthly contribution between them.
- The money, about a day after the call, with nobody deciding:
  - an "available now" call: the time both were in the call, whoever ended it;
  - a booked call or a call paid from a membership, ended by the host: the host's time in the
    call up to that moment;
  - ended by the customer: his whole booked time. That is the time the host was in the call
    before that moment, plus the rest of the booking. Time he waited for the host is not charged.
    If the host was not in the call when he ended it, the rest of the booking is not charged.
    If he ended it before the booked start, it is charged as his cancelling at that moment would
    have been;
  - ended by the customer, who then reports the host from that call before the money is taken:
    only the time the call lasted, as if the host had ended it.
- A report by a host changes nothing about the money. No report can raise a charge.
- A customer whose reports after ending a call have changed a call from "the whole booking" to
  "the minutes" 3 times among his last 5 booked calls: a further report no longer lowers his charge. His report and the block still go
  through. He is told at once. The founder is told once.
- Nothing is charged under 50 cents. The refund request to the host, the host's refund and
  Connect's refund all remain.

## 1. Community Rules

**Under "If someone breaks the rules", in place of the line about a call:**

> - **On a call:** press **"End call now"**. The call ends for both of you at once, and nobody
>   can come back in. The next screen lets you report the other person, block them, or do
>   neither. Reporting someone also blocks them.

**A new rule, or a sentence in the rule about honesty:**

> **Report only what happened.** A report must be true. Making a report you know to be untrue,
> for example to pay less for a call, breaks these rules and can mean a paused or closed account.

## 2. Terms of Service

**Section 7.5, in place of the present text:**

> 7.5 **Ending a call.** Either person can end a call at once with "End call now". The call ends
> for both, and nobody can rejoin it. You can then report the other person, block them, or do
> neither. What is charged for a call ended this way is set out in the Payment Terms, section 8.
> Reporting a person blocks them for you. Blocking a person also cancels your other calls with
> them that are still to come, at no charge, and ends any membership or monthly contribution
> between the two of you. They are told that those have ended, never that you blocked them.

## 3. Payment Terms

**Section 8, in place of the present text (its heading becomes "A call that one of you ends"):**

> 8.1 If either person ends a call with "End call now", time after that moment does not count.
>
> 8.2 **An instant call** is charged as always: the time you were both connected.
>
> 8.3 **A call booked for a later time, or paid from a membership:**
>
> - **If the host ends it,** you pay for the time the host was in the call up to that moment.
> - **If you end it,** you pay for your whole booked time: the time the host was in the call
>   before that moment, and the rest of the time you booked. You are told this when you book, and
>   the app says it again and asks you to confirm before the call is ended. You do not pay for time you waited for the host, and if
>   the host was not in the call when you ended it you do not pay for the rest of the booking.
>   If you end it before its booked start, it is charged as a cancellation at that moment would
>   be (section 6).
> - **If you end it because of something the host did, and you report the host from that call
>   before the money is taken** (about a day after the call), you pay only for the time the call
>   lasted, as if the host had ended it. A report made later does not change the charge by
>   itself; you can still ask for a refund (section 9).
>
> 8.4 Time you added during the call follows the same rule.
>
> 8.5 A report must be true (Community Rules). To protect hosts, we look at how often a
> customer's reports after ending a call have lowered what they paid. If that has happened 3
> times among your last 5 booked calls, a further report made after ending a call does not lower
> the charge for that call. Your report is still read and the person is still blocked, and we
> tell you at once. You can ask for a refund as always.
>
> 8.6 We do not decide these charges one by one. Nothing here limits a refund we or the host
> choose to give, or a right the law gives you.

## 4. Host Agreement

**Section 8.3, the first ground in the list ("the call was ended with 'End call and report', by
either person") is taken out, and a new section says what happens instead:**

> 8.x **A call that one of you ends.** If you end a booked call with "End call now", you are paid
> for your time in the call up to that moment. If the customer ends it, they are charged their
> whole booked time (your time in the call, and the rest of the booking), unless you were not in
> the call at that moment. If the customer ends it and then reports you from that call before
> the money is taken, they are charged only for your time in the call up to that moment, as if
> you had ended it. This is applied automatically: we do not examine the report before the
> charge is made, and you are not told who reported you. The most this can cost you is the
> difference between the whole booking and the time you gave. A customer whose reports have
> lowered their charge several times loses that for further calls (Payment Terms, section 8.5).
> If you believe a report about you was untrue, write to us.

**Section 8.3 keeps its other grounds**, among them a charge "for a call that broke the Community
Rules", which covers a refund we decide after reading a report.

## 5. Ratings (Terms of Service, the paragraph on ratings)

Added on 27 November 2026 (plan 51, decision 27). The draft says "Cancelled calls, safety-ended
calls and calls needing attendance review cannot be rated". The app now lets both people rate a
call that one of them ended with "End call now", under the same rules as any other call (both in
the call together for at least two minutes, a final record, seven days from the booked end). Only
a call ended with the old button, "End call and report", stays without ratings, and no such call
is known outside test data. Proposed:

> Cancelled calls and calls needing attendance review cannot be rated. A call that one of you
> ended with "End call now" can be rated like any other.
