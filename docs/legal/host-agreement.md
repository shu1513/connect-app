## The short version

This box is a summary to help you. It is not the agreement; the numbered sections are.

- **You work for yourself.** You are not our employee. You choose your price, your hours and
  whom you talk to.
- **The call is your service to your customer.** We run the website and take the customer's
  payment for you.
- **You are paid your price less our fee.** Today the fee is 4.9% plus 30 cents for each paid
  call, plus 1.5% when the customer's card is from outside the United States, and 25 cents each
  time you collect your earnings. You keep every tip in full.
- **You decide refund requests.** If a customer's bank takes money back after you were paid, you
  owe it back.
- **You handle your own taxes.**
- **Pay for Connect calls on Connect only.**
- We can pause or close a host page that breaks the rules. If you are in the United Kingdom or

## 2. Becoming and staying a host

2.1 **Applying.** To host you must: be 18 or over; pass the identity check; show us that you

2.3 **Your payout account.** To be paid you open a payout account with Stripe and accept

and you cannot take paid calls.

2.4 **Keep it true.** Your name, your description, your languages, your photo and your social
accounts must be true and yours, and must stay so. If you change your name or description we
may look at your page again before the change is shown.

2.5 **One person.** A host page belongs to one real person: you. You take every call yourself.
You may not let anyone else take a call in your place, and you may not host for someone else's
name or account. If a manager or an agency helps you, you remain the host, you remain
responsible, and you promise that you have every right to offer calls under the name and the
social accounts on your page.

2.6 **Equipment.** You provide your own device, camera, microphone, internet connection and a

4.3 **Cancel early if you must.** If you cancel a call the customer pays nothing. Cancel as

4.4 **Be honest about yourself.** Do not claim a qualification, a licence, an experience or a

4.7 **Safety.** If something on a call breaks the Community Rules, end it with "End call and

## 6. Prices and our fees

6.1 **Your price.** You set a price for each 15 minutes. The lowest price the Service allows is
shown when you set it (today US $5.00 for each 15 minutes). A member price may not be higher
than your regular price. Prices are in US dollars.

6.2 **Our fee on calls, memberships and contributions.** From each payment a customer makes for
your call, membership month or project, we keep:

- 4.9% of the amount charged; and
- a fixed 30 cents for each separate card charge (a call is one charge, and each stretch of
  time added during a call is another; for a membership, once for each month and not for each
  call; for a contribution, for each payment); and
- 1.5% more of the amount charged when the customer's card was issued outside the United
  States.

The rest is your share. Your page for earnings shows the split for every payment.

6.3 **Tips.** You receive the whole of every tip. We keep nothing of it. The customer pays the
cost of the card payment on top of the tip.

6.4 **The collection charge.** Each time you collect your earnings we keep 25 cents, which
covers what sending the money costs. It is taken once for each collection, not for each call.

6.5 **Refunds.** When a payment is refunded in full, our fee is returned too. When part is
refunded, your share and our fee shrink in the same proportion.

6.6 **No other fees.** We charge you no joining fee and no monthly fee.

6.7 **Changing our fees.** We may change our fees. We tell you by email at least 30 days before
a change takes effect. A change never applies to a call that was booked, or a membership month
that began, before it took effect. If you do not accept a change you may stop hosting before it
takes effect.

## 7. When and how you are paid

7.1 **You collect.** Your share is not sent by itself. When money is ready, you press "Collect"
on your earnings page, and it is sent to your Stripe payout account; Stripe then pays your
bank. You can collect once in any 24 hours, when more than 25 cents is ready.

7.2 **When money is ready.** Your earnings page shows, for each payment, when it will be ready
and why it is not ready yet. Today:

- **A call:** 24 hours after the call ends. If the customer asked for a refund, 2 days after
  the call ends and not before you have answered the request.
- **A membership:** your share for time a member used is ready as for a call. Your share of
  time that expired unused is ready after the month has ended.
- **A contribution or a tip:** 7 days after the money was taken from the customer's card. If
  one person has given you more than US $1,000 in 30 days, that person's money is ready 30 days
  after it was taken.

7.3 **Nothing is ready before the money exists.** A customer's card is first only held. Until
the money has been taken from the card, there is nothing to collect.

7.4 **When we may hold money back.** We may hold a payment, or all of your earnings, for as
long as is reasonably needed, where:

- a customer's bank has opened a dispute or asked a question about a payment to you;
- a refund request for that call is open;
- we have good reason to suspect fraud, a breach of this agreement or of the law, or that a
  payment will be reversed;
- your account is paused or closed and we are settling what is owed in each direction;
- Stripe or a court or an authority requires it; or
- you owe us money (section 9).

We tell you when we hold money and why, unless the law forbids it. A hold for a suspected
breach lasts no longer than 180 days unless a dispute, an investigation or the law requires
longer.

7.5 **Other currencies.** If your bank account is not in US dollars, Stripe converts the money
at its own rate when it pays you. We do not control that rate.

7.6 **Check your earnings.** Tell us within 60 days if you think a payment to you is wrong or
missing. After that we may treat the record as correct, unless the law gives you longer.

7.7 **Money you do not collect.** Earnings stay recorded for you until you collect them. They
remain yours: not collecting them, or having your page closed, does not make them ours, and we

have not collected for [12] months, we may send what is ready to your payout account ourselves.

## 8. Cancellations, missed calls and refunds

8.1 **The customer's rules.** The Payment Terms say what a customer is charged, what cancelling

- a customer who is late or does not join pays for the time you were there waiting during the
  booked time (for an instant call, only for time you were both there).

8.2 **Refund requests are yours to decide.** A customer can ask for a refund of a call once,
within 24 hours of its start. You have 24 hours to answer: charge in full, charge less, or
charge nothing. **If you do not answer in time, the customer is charged as usual.** After the
money has been taken, you can still give a refund from your earnings page.

8.3 **When we decide instead of you.** We may decide a refund in your place, and may refund a
customer whatever you answered, where:

- the call was ended with "End call and report", by either person;
- you did not join, or the law or the rules of the card companies require a refund;
- we find that the charge was made by mistake, by fraud, or for a call that broke the Community
  Rules;
- your account is paused or closed, or you cannot be reached; or
- a customer in the United Kingdom or Europe uses a legal right to cancel or to a remedy.

8.4 **Tips and contributions.** Once the money of a tip or a contribution has been taken, giving
it back is your decision, with the same exceptions as in 8.3.

8.5 **A refund comes out of your share and our fee together,** as section 6.5 says. If you had
already collected your share, you owe it back to us (section 9).

## 9. Bank disputes, and money you owe us

9.1 **Bank disputes.** A customer can ask their bank to reverse a payment (a "chargeback"). The
bank decides, not us. While a dispute is open, the money for that payment cannot be collected.
We may answer the bank with the records we have, and you agree to send us, within 5 days of
our asking, anything true that you have and that helps. Do not contact the customer to press
them to withdraw a dispute, and do not try to collect the money from them yourself.

9.2 **If the bank takes the money back,** the payment is undone: your share of it is no longer
yours. If you had already collected it, you owe it back to us. We bear the bank's own dispute
fee unless the dispute was caused by your breach of this agreement, in which case you owe us
that fee too.

9.3 **Other money you owe us.** You also owe us: any refund under section 8 of money you had
already collected; any amount we paid you by mistake; and any amount due under section 16 (tax we had to pay for you) or section 18.2.

9.4 **How we recover it.** We tell you in writing which payment it is about, why, and how the
amount is worked out, and you have 14 days to tell us if you think it is wrong. We may take
what you owe us from your later earnings before you collect them. If your earnings in the following 30 days do not cover it, we may send you a
request to pay, and you agree to pay within 30 days. We may ask Stripe to take it from your
payout account where Stripe's agreement with you allows.

9.5 **A membership and a dispute.** A bank dispute, or a bank's question, about a membership
payment ends that membership.

## 10. Memberships

10.1 **Your offer.** You may offer one membership: a member price for each 15 minutes and an
amount of time each month. You may invite only people you have already had a call with.

10.2 **What you owe a member.** A member pays you each month for time with you. You must offer
enough bookable hours for your members to use the time they paid for. The Service will not let
the time of all your members together be more than the hours you offer in four weeks.

10.3 **Time you take away.** If you cancel a member's call, the time goes back to the member.
If they cannot use it before the month ends, its price is paid back to them, out of your share
and our fee.

10.4 **Unused time.** Time a member does not use by the end of a month expires. Your share of
it becomes ready after the month ends.

10.5 **Changing your offer.** A change applies to new invitations. People who are already
members keep their time, and their price can only go down, never up. You cannot change a
member's price or time during a month they have paid for.

10.6 **Cutting your hours.** If you cut your hours so that not every member still fits, you
choose who keeps their place, who is offered less time, and who is paused after their current
month. Months that have started are never touched.

10.7 **Ending a membership.** If you end a membership, the member's unused time for the current
month is paid back to them. If your host page is suspended or your account is paused or closed,
your memberships end and unused time is paid back in the same way.
