# Messages between hosts and customers: proposed wording

Written on 26 November 2026 by the builder, for plan 49 (`plans/49-messaging.md`, decision 32).
**Nothing here is in force, and none of it was written or checked by a lawyer.** It is proposed
wording for three of the documents in `src/legal/documents/`, for a lawyer to read and correct.

**Why it is here and not in the app.** A change to the Terms of Service or the Community Rules is
a new version, and a new version sends every account back to the welcome step to accept again.
That should happen once, on purpose, with the lawyer's words, and together with the other changes
that are waiting (see `docs/for-the-lawyer.md`). Until then the app's documents do not mention
messages. The app itself is ahead of them: messages are built and switched on.

The open questions behind this wording are in `docs/for-the-lawyer.md`, section 8.

## 1. Community Rules

**A new sentence at the top, after the first paragraph:**

> These rules hold everywhere on Connect: on a call, in a request's note, on a host's page and
> diary, and in the messages a host and a customer write to each other.

**In rule 11 (no spam and no misuse), add:**

> Do not use messages to advertise, to send the same thing to many people, or to keep writing to
> someone who does not answer.

**A new rule, after the rule about paying outside Connect:**

> **Keep calls and payments for them on Connect.** Do not use messages to move a call, or the
> payment for one, somewhere else, and do not ask for or send contact details for that purpose.

**Under "If someone breaks the rules", a new line:**

> - **In a message:** press "Report" beside the message and send the form that opens. The other
>   person is then blocked, and we keep a copy of that part of the conversation to look at.

**And a correction that has nothing to do with messages** (plan 49, "Found on the way"). The line
about a call says today: "The call ends for both of you, the other person is blocked, and the
money for the call waits for our review." The app blocks only when the report form that follows
is sent. Until the app is changed, the true sentence is:

> - **On a call:** press **"End call and report"**. The call ends for both of you, and the money
>   for the call waits for our review. When you send the report form that follows, the other
>   person is blocked.

The Terms of Service make the same promise in their section on ending a call, and need the same
correction.

## 2. Terms of Service

**A new section, "Messages":**

> **Messages between a host and a customer.**
>
> (a) *When you can write.* A host and a customer can write to each other from the moment a call
> between them is agreed until 7 days after it. After that, a host can write to a customer she
> has had a call with, and the customer can answer for 7 days after her latest message. A
> customer who wants to write to a host again books another call. We can change these times, and
> we limit how many messages a person can send.
>
> (b) *Switching a host off.* A customer can stop a host from writing to them between calls, in
> the conversation with that host. Around a call the customer has booked with her, the two can
> still write. Anyone can block anyone.
>
> (c) *What messages are.* Messages are plain text. They are stored by Connect on its servers.
> **They are not end-to-end encrypted:** Connect is able to read them.
>
> (d) *When we read them.* We do not read your conversations as a matter of routine, and we do
> not scan them. When one of the two people reports a message, we keep a copy of that part of the
> conversation with the report, and our staff read it to decide what to do. Every such look is
> recorded.
>
> (e) *When we hand them on.* We give messages to the authorities when the law requires it, for
> example when a report shows that a child is being exploited, and we keep what we have reported
> for as long as the law requires.
>
> (f) *No promise that a message is read.* We do not send an email when a message arrives. The
> other person sees it when they next open Connect. Do not use messages for anything urgent.

## 3. Privacy Policy

**In the section on what we collect, a new line:**

> - **Messages you write to a host or a customer on Connect,** with the time each was sent.

**In the section on who can see what:**

> - **Your messages** are seen by you and the one person you wrote to. Our staff see a part of a
>   conversation only when one of you has reported a message in it; each look is recorded with
>   who looked and when.

**In the section on how long we keep things:**

> - **A message:** 12 months after it was sent, then it is deleted. Your choice to stop a host
>   from writing to you is kept for as long as your account is.
> - **A copy kept with a report:** for as long as the report is kept. [LAWYER: how long that must
>   and may be; at least one year from our own report where we have reported to the authorities.]

**In the section on sharing with others:**

> - **The authorities,** where the law requires us to report or to hand something over.
